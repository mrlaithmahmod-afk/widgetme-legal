# GTM, Channel/Reseller Strategy and SaaS Financial Benchmarks: UK AI Property Visualisation SaaS (as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: researched 20 Oct 2026 with a limited tool budget (about 20 searches/fetches). Many benchmark figures come from vendor blogs that summarise primary reports (SaaS Capital, ICONIQ, Benchmarkit, ChartMogul). Those are flagged as secondary. Anything labelled **[ESTIMATE]** or **[ASSUMPTION]** is the researcher's own judgement, not a sourced fact.

## UK channels: events, associations, software marketplaces, data/lead sources, partner networks

### Takeaway
The cheapest UK routes to market are integration marketplaces, Reapit's Foundations/AppMarket above all: agents install free, partners set their own pricing, and developer-network membership was reported at about £1,500+ a year. Major events are expensive. A public-sector UKREiiF pavilion package cost £39.5k + VAT for 2026, and a small generic UK show stand budgets about £10k. For a startup, attending, speaking or sponsoring a fringe slot will likely pay back better than its own stand. Developer-pipeline data (Barbour ABI) costs about £4k–£11k a year.

### Cited Findings
**Events**
- UKREiiF 2026: East Riding of Yorkshire Council awarded a "2026 UKREiiF Pavilion" package at £39,500 ex VAT (£47,400 inc VAT), contract 21 Oct 2025–21 May 2026. This is a shared regional investment pavilion, not a standard stand — [Find a Tender notice 064887-2025](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/064887-2025)
- A West Midlands UKREiiF pavilion tender estimated £45,000 ex VAT for the temporary structure lot alone (200 sqm), with £175,000 ex VAT total estimated value across lots — [Find a Tender notice 065661-2025](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/065661-2025/PDF)
- Generic UK sample budget for a 12 sqm stand at a major show: about £9,700 including space, build, power, furniture and storage. Vendor guide, not UKREiiF-specific — [Booth Exhibits](https://boothexhibits.com/?p=19816)
- No published exhibitor rate cards were found for UKREiiF, MIPIM, Future of London, Housing 2026, RESI, The Negotiator Awards, Proptech Connect or BTR events (see Gaps).

**Estate-agency software marketplaces**
- Reapit AppMarket: each partner sets its own pricing and must show it before install. Some Reapit apps are free — [Reapit AppMarket FAQ](https://reapit.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RW/pages/923598951/AppMarket+Frequently+Asked+Questions)

- Reapit Foundations partner programme page — [Reapit Foundations](https://www.reapit.com/foundations/)
- A third-party developer reported that joining the Reapit developer network costs "£1500+ per annum". Secondhand and undated — [WP Property Feed knowledge base](https://wppropertyfeed.co.uk/knowledge-base/category/feeds)
- Rightmove is a strategic investor in Coadjute, the agent transaction-tracking network. Lloyds put £3m into a £10m round in 2024, with Nationwide and NatWest also investing, and Coadjute has raised £23m in total since 2018 — [UKTN](https://www.uktech.news/proptech/coadjute-proptech-funding-lloyds-20240402); [Estate Agent Today](https://www.estateagenttoday.co.uk/breaking-news/2024/4/proptech-firm-secures-10m-of-strategic-investment)

**Developer-pipeline data sources**
- Barbour ABI: average subscriptions start at about £4k and are tailored by sector, region and planning stage. 10 free leads are offered — [Barbour ABI](https://barbour-abi.com/construction-leads/get-10-free-leads/)
- A Barbour ABI ROI page assumes an average annual subscription of about £7,500 — [Barbour ABI](https://barbour-abi.com/?p=11854)

### Inferences
- [ESTIMATE] A realistic year-1 events budget for a seed-stage UK startup is £15k–£40k. That covers UKREiiF and RESI delegate passes, one or two fringe sponsorships or awards-table bookings, and possibly a small stand at a proptech show. A UKREiiF pavilion-level presence (about £40k+) only makes sense co-funded with partners.
- [INFERENCE] Reapit AppMarket fits the agent segment best. Agents install free, so friction is low, and partners keep their own pricing. Alto (Zoopla/Houseful), Street.co.uk, Jupix, Loop and Agent OS integrations should come second, after demand is validated. Rightmove's investment in Coadjute shows portals backing workflow networks, which is a possible future partnership channel.
- [INFERENCE] For developers, Barbour ABI or Glenigan (about £4k–£11k a year) plus free planning-portal and Companies House scraping can build a list of active schemes. LandTech/LandInsight, EGi and Molior suit land and London-specific intelligence (pricing not found).

## Reseller/white-label economics and partner programmes in comparable proptech

### Takeaway
Comparable proptech vendors (Matterport, CubiCasa) do not publish their reseller margins. Matterport's programme offers hardware discounts, a "SaaS recruiting commission" and special SaaS rates, in return for annual revenue commitments. No reliable public evidence was found to confirm the 20–40% reseller margin range.

### Cited Findings
- The Matterport Channel Partner programme is "only for companies interested in reselling Matterport Pro2 cameras and plans". Benefits include Pro2/Pro3 camera discounts and a "Matterport SaaS Recruiting Commission". Partners commit to an annual revenue commitment and growth targets under a cooperative agreement — [Matterport Channel Partner page](https://www.matterport.com/en-gb/node/1443); [Matterport become a partner](https://matterport.com/ko/partners/become-a-partner)
- Matterport's VAR programme expansion (EMEA/APAC/LATAM) offered "special rates on hardware and SaaS plans and demo equipment", without disclosing the rates — [Matterport news](https://matterport.com/news/matterport-expands-its-value-added-reseller-var-program-emea-apac-and-latam)
- CubiCasa runs an MLS partnership programme (free app-generated floor plans for members, discounted add-ons, a photographer directory), not a published white-label or reseller programme. Consumer tiers are listed as Lite $0, Plus $15 and Plus 3D $65 per order, with surcharges above 5,000 sq ft. CubiCasa was acquired by Clear Capital in September 2021 — [RISMedia](https://www.rismedia.com/2023/07/26/10-mlss-partner-with-cubicasa/); [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/cubicasa/pricing)
- Giraffe360 (London, hardware + AI media kits for photographers and agents) raised a Series B of about $10m (€8.7m) in March 2026, led by Cipio Partners with Founders Fund and Hoxton Ventures. Amounts vary between trackers — [Traded](https://traded.co/deals/national/all/sale/giraffe360-raises-10-million-in-series-b-round-led-by-cipio-partners-and-other-investors/); [tech.eu](https://funding.tech.eu/companies/AD4C0B56-1FB6-4F82-ABE1-F4A8F3A4D144). An earlier round was about $16m led by Founders Fund plus $6m venture debt (Columbia Lake) — [Giraffe360](https://giraffe360.com/insights/giraffe360-closes-second-investment-round)

### Inferences
- [ASSUMPTION, label in model] Model reseller discounts of 20–30% off list for agencies and photographers, and 10–20% recurring referral commission for 12 months. These are common B2B SaaS norms in the researcher's general knowledge, but **not verified** by a 2025–26 source here.
- [INFERENCE] Matterport's model (volume commitment in exchange for discount, with commission on SaaS recruited) is a workable template for channel-conflict control. Tier discounts on committed volume, and use deal registration so direct sales do not undercut partners.
- [INFERENCE] White-label viewers for agencies and CGI studios are best priced as a platform fee plus usage, with the agency owning the client relationship. This reduces conflict with the direct developer motion.

### Gaps
- No public BoxBrownie white-label or partner terms, and no Giraffe360 reseller terms, were found.
- No survey evidence was found on whether CGI studios adopt AI tools internally rather than resell them. This needs primary interviews.
- No verified 2025–26 source was found for typical B2B SaaS reseller margins or referral commission rates.

## Outbound benchmarks (LinkedIn/email response rates)

### Takeaway
Cold email replies typically run about 1–5% (often cited near 3%). LinkedIn connection acceptance is about 28% on average (VP+ about 22%), first-message replies after accepting are about 10%, and InMail response is about 6% in Belkins' 2025 study. Nearly all of these figures are vendor-published.

### Cited Findings
- Cold email reply rates are typically 1–5%. LinkedIn connection acceptance averaged about 28.5% across about 13M requests, with targeted B2B campaigns at 30–45%. Replies to notes attached to connection requests are about 2.2–3%. InMail response is quoted at 10–25% — [Salesflow](https://salesflow.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-templates)
- Belkins' 2025 LinkedIn study reported a 6.38% overall InMail response rate. Cold email is quoted near 3% — [Gracker](https://gracker.ai/blog/linkedin-inmail-vs-connection-requests); primary report: [Belkins 2025 B2B cold outreach benchmarks](https://belkins.io/resources/b2b-cold-outreach-benchmarks-2025-report)
- Acceptance rate by seniority: VP+ about 22% vs individual contributors about 41% — [GetFuzzy](https://getfuzzy.ai/blog/linkedin-outreach-statistics)
- Average reply to the first message after acceptance is about 10.4% (Expandi data) — [Overloop](https://overloop.com/blog/linkedin-outreach-benchmarks)
- For the same recipients, cold email replied at about 4.4% versus 16.6% on LinkedIn. Acceptance varies by industry, from 17.5% to 40.1% — [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/?p=7548)

### Inferences
- [ASSUMPTION for model] Per 1,000 developer or agency decision-makers contacted: about 25–30 email replies or about 22% acceptance × 10% reply on LinkedIn (about 22 replies). About 30–40% of replies become meetings, so roughly 8–12 meetings per 1,000 contacts. These conversion ratios are researcher estimates.

### Gaps
- No UK property-sector-specific outbound benchmarks were found.

## SaaS benchmarks for the financial model

### Takeaway
SaaS Capital's 2025 survey (1,000+ private B2B companies over $1M ARR) gives median NRR of about 102% at $25–50k ACV and median GRR of about 91% below $250k ACV. CAC payback medians are about 15–16 months, shorter (about 9 months) for ACV under $5k. AI products run materially lower gross margins: ICONIQ reports about 45–52% for AI products vs about 80% for traditional software. Trial conversion depends heavily on whether a card is required.

### Cited Findings
**Retention / churn**
- SaaS Capital's 2025 survey (14th annual, more than 1,000 private B2B SaaS companies, excluding firms under $1M ARR) found median GRR of about 91% for ACV under $250k and 95% above $250k. At $25k–$50k ACV, median NRR is 102% (top quartile 111%, bottom 97%). At least a quarter of companies in every band under $250k see net contraction. Median growth for companies over $1M ARR is 24% — [SaaS Capital retention benchmarks](https://www.saas-capital.com/research/saas-retention-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-companies/); [2025 PDF brief](https://www.saas-capital.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/RB32WS1-2025-B2B-SaaS-Retention-Benchmarks.pdf)
- Median ACV rises with NRR: $21,017 for NRR under 90%, $24,299 at 90–100%, and $44,073 at 100–110% — [SaaS Capital](https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/what-is-a-good-retention-rate-for-a-private-saas-company/)

**CAC payback / sales cycle**
- Median B2B SaaS CAC payback was 16 months in 2025, down from 18 in 2024 (Aleph × Benchmarkit) — [Aleph](https://www.getaleph.com/answers/cac-payback-period-saas-2026). Optifai puts the median at 15 months (939 companies), and Benchmarkit 2024 data gives about 9 months for ACV under $5k vs 24 months for ACV over $100k. PLG motions run 6–12 months, sales-assisted SMB/mid-market 12–18 — [SaaS Hero](https://saashero.net/strategy/cac-payback-benchmarks-arr-stage/) (secondary)
- Sales cycle: enterprise averages about 180 days, smaller deals about 75 days. Not split by ACV, and the source is a secondary blog — [Sales-Mind](https://sales-mind.ai/blog/post/kpi-benchmarks-saas-sales-teams-2025)

**Gross margin (AI-heavy)**
- ICONIQ State of AI (via SaaStr): AI product gross margin averaged about 45% in 2025, projected at 53% for 2026 and 59% for 2027. Another ICONIQ release cites 52%, up from 41% — [SaaStr on ICONIQ](https://saastr.com/whats-truly-great-now-in-b2b-ai-per-iconiq-115-growth-at-100m-55-gross-margins-and-655k-in-revenue-per-employee); [SaaStr on ICONIQ State of Software 2025](https://www.saastr.com/iconiqs-state-of-software-in-2025-much-smaller-teams-55-of-gtm-is-now-in-post-sales-2025-is-crushing-2024-for-funding)
- Secondary framing: AI companies at about 50–60% GM vs 70–90% for mature SaaS. Bessemer "Supernovas" average about 25% and "Shooting Stars" about 60%. Median software GM was about 80% in FY2025 — [CloudZero](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/ai-gross-margin); [Orb](https://www.withorb.com/blog/saas-growth-margin-statistics); [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/ai-startup-growth/ai-vs-saas-gross-margins)

**Trial / freemium conversion**
- Opt-out (card required) trials convert at 48.8% vs 18.2% for opt-in (no card), from First Page Sage client data (86 companies). ChartMogul 2026 figures, as tabulated by Userpilot: no-card median about 4–6% (P75 10–15%), card-required median 25–35% (P75 50–60%) — [Userpilot](https://userpilot.com/blog/free-trial-conversion-rate/)
- Median B2B trial-to-paid conversion is 18.5%, with top performers at 35–45% — [Flint](https://www.flint.com/blog/b2b-saas-free-trial-conversion-rate-statistics). A January 2026 report on 200 products found an 8% median with a bimodal distribution — [Shno](https://www.shno.co/marketing-statistics/free-trial-conversion-statistics). Freemium converts at about 2–5%, and sales-assisted trials at 25–40% — [Sleek](https://www.getsleek.io/blog/saas-analytics-benchmarks-2026)

### Inferences
- [ASSUMPTION for model] Monthly logo churn of about 2.5–3.5% for SMB estate agents (self-serve, ACV under £3k). For annual mid-market developer contracts (£10k–£50k ACV), GRR is about 88–92% a year (about 0.7–1.0% monthly), consistent with SaaS Capital's about 91% GRR for ACV under $250k. Base-case NRR is 95–102%, upside 105–110% where developers add schemes and configurators.
- [ASSUMPTION] Blended gross margin of 55–65% in year 1, rising to 65–75% by year 3 as GPU and inference costs fall and human QA is automated. If human CGI artists stay in the loop, the business behaves more like a tech-enabled service (about 40–55%).
- [ASSUMPTION] CAC payback of 6–12 months for the agent self-serve or Reapit-marketplace motion, and 14–20 months for the developer sales-led motion.

### Gaps
- Not found within budget: KeyBanc/Sapphire, OpenView (ceased operations in 2025, so its last benchmarks date from 2023–24), High Alpha, Paddle/ProfitWell monthly churn by segment, or win-rate benchmarks.
- No direct a16z AI gross-margin figure was retrieved.
- No sourced data was found on time from launch to $1m ARR for bootstrapped vertical SaaS.

## UK operating costs (salaries, insurance, compliance)

### Takeaway
UK senior engineers cost about £70k–£100k base nationally and £85k–£130k in London. A first ISO 27001 certification costs a 10–50 person firm about £12k–£25k all-in (auditor fees £4k–£8k). Cyber insurance premiums are not public; the ABI average claim rose to over £276k in 2024.

### Cited Findings
- Henderson Scott Technology Salary Guide 2026: senior software engineer base is £70k–£90k (Java) and £75k–£100k (Python). Senior contractors earn about £400–£600 a day outside IR35 — [Henderson Scott 2026 guide (PDF)](https://www.hendersonscott.co.uk/media/11zjl0na/hs-technology-salary-guide-2026.pdf)
- Senior full-stack pay of £70k–£100k; London seniors at about £85k–£130k, and £110k–£160k+ at big tech and top fintech — [Digital Waffle](https://www.digitalwaffle.co/salary-guides/software-engineering-salaries); [UK NetPay](https://uknetpay.co.uk/insights/software-engineer-salary-uk-2026); [Shashi Works](https://www.shashiworks.com/software-engineer-jobs-london.html) (secondary)
- ISO 27001 first-year cost for a 10–50 person UK firm is £12k–£25k, with certification-body fees of £4k–£8k for Stage 1 and Stage 2 — [Pyralink (May 2026)](https://pyralink.co.uk/insights/2026-05-26-iso-27001-cost-uk-certification/). The average UK auditor day rate is about £1,250, so about £6,250 for a 1–10 person firm — [HighTable](https://hightable.io/iso-27001-costs-explained-simply). Surveillance audits run about 30–40% of the initial fee each year on a 3-year cycle — [HighTable](https://hightable.io/iso-27001-3-year-cost-cycle-guide)
- Vanta (US): overall cost of $6k–$40k+, with Stage 1+2 audits at about $14k–$16k — [Vanta](https://www.vanta.com/collection/iso-27001/iso-27001-certification-cost). The competitor Strac costs from $4,995 a year for up to 10 employees, with the audit billed separately — [Strac](https://www.strac.io/blog/iso-27001-certification-cost)
- UK SME cyber-insurance penetration: micro firms 26.4%, small 40.1%, medium 63%. The ABI average cyber claim paid exceeded £276k in 2024, up from under £113k in 2023 — [GlobalData](https://www.globaldata.com/media/insurance/uk-cyber-insurance-adoption-lags-despite-surge-in-attacks-finds-globaldata/)

### Inferences
- [ASSUMPTION for model; employer on-costs] Add about 15% employer NIC (15% from April 2025) plus 3–5% pension and benefits, roughly 18–22% on top of base salary.
- [ESTIMATE] Combined cyber + PI + public liability for a £1–5m-limit early-stage SaaS is about £2k–£6k a year. This is unsourced, so get broker quotes.
- [ESTIMATE] Compliance platform (Vanta/Drata-class) at about £5k–£12k a year plus the auditor. Year-1 ISO 27001 at about £15k–£25k, then about £8k–£12k a year after.

### Gaps
- No sourced UK figures found for AE/SDR/CSM salaries, graphics/3D engineer premiums, or ONS/Hays/Otta data.
- No public UK cyber/PI premium benchmarks were found, and no current Vanta/Drata list prices.
- UK company formation and accounting costs were not researched. Companies House incorporation fee is £100 since May 2024 (researcher's knowledge, unverified here).

## Acquirer landscape and revenue multiples

### Takeaway
The anchor deal is CoStar–Matterport: about $1.6bn, closed 28 Feb 2025, at about 9–10x trailing revenue (researcher's calculation). REA's rejected 2024 approaches for Rightmove peaked at about £6.2bn. Private SaaS medians in 2026 are about 3–5x ARR, with bootstrapped companies at about 4.8x and VC-backed at about 5.3x (SaaS Capital 2025 model).

### Cited Findings
- CoStar completed its acquisition of Matterport on 28 Feb 2025, ahead of schedule, at about $1.6bn: $5.50 a share ($2.75 cash + 0.03552 CoStar shares). The price was far below Matterport's nearly $10bn post-2021-IPO valuation — [CoStar 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1057352/000105735225000067/csgp-20250331.htm); [Inman](https://news2.inman.com/2025/02/28/costar-closes-1-6b-matterport-acquisition-ahead-of-schedule/); [CoStar announcement](https://www.costar.com/article/628706126/costar-group-to-acquire-matterport-global-leader-in-immersive-3d-digital-twins-and-artificial-intelligence-for-the-real-estate-industry)
- Matterport FY2023 revenue was about $158m and FY2024 about $169.7m, implying about 10.1x and 9.4x revenue. Revenue figures come from the search summary and the multiple is the researcher's calculation, so verify against filings — [Construction Dive](https://www.constructiondive.com/news/costar-deal-matterport-ai-reality-capture/714360/)
- REA Group made four approaches for Rightmove in Sept 2024. The fourth (about 775p, about £6.2bn) was rejected on 30 Sept 2024 — [Online Marketplaces](https://www.onlinemarketplaces.com/articles/rightmove-rejects-fourth-takeover-bid-from-rea-group-ahead-of-todays-put-up-or-shut-up-deadline/); [NST/Reuters](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/property/2024/09/1112879/uks-rightmove-rejects-us829-bln-proposal-murdoch-owned-rea-group). Rightmove's FY2025 revenue was reported at £425.1m (+9%) — same source.
- Private SaaS multiples in 2026: median about 4.5x ARR (range 3–7x). Companies with $0–5M ARR sit at about 3.3x (Aventis Advisors). SaaS Capital's 2025 model puts bootstrapped $3–20M ARR companies at about 4.8x and VC-backed at 5.3x. Companies with Rule of 40 above 50 and NRR above 120% close at 7–9x. All of these are secondary aggregations — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/saas-valuation-statistics-2026/); [Peony](https://www.peony.ink/blog/saas-valuation-multiples); [Livmo](https://livmo.com/blog/saas-valuation-multiples-2026/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Likely acquirers include portals (Rightmove, Zoopla/Houseful, CoStar/OnTheMarket), agency CRMs (Reapit, Apex27, Alto), visual-media platforms (Matterport/CoStar, Giraffe360, BoxBrownie, Esoft) and data firms (Hometrack). These are strategic buyers that could pay above the about 3–5x private median for strong UK distribution.
- [ASSUMPTION for exit scenarios] For a sub-£5m ARR UK vertical SaaS: 3–5x ARR base case, 6–8x with over 110% NRR and over 40% growth.

### Gaps
- Not verified within budget: Reapit ownership changes, Apex27 or Vitec deals, Esoft, Zoopla/Houseful transactions, Hometrack, BoxBrownie funding, or OnTheMarket (acquired by CoStar in Dec 2023, from the researcher's knowledge, unverified here).
- No primary vertical-SaaS M&A multiple dataset was retrieved; the SaaS Capital Index and Aventis originals should be checked directly.
